LLM Vendor Fees are metered from the provider usage the Platform records for Customer's requests and billed in arrears, separately from the platform fee.

(b) Customer's own key. When Customer connects a model provider with its own account or API key, the provider charges Customer directly under Customer's agreement with it, and no LLM Vendor Fees apply to that usage. Any Meibel charge for using a customer-supplied key is stated in the Price List.

(c) Price changes. Changes in a provider's own prices flow through to LLM Vendor Fees when they take effect. A change Meibel makes to how LLM Vendor Fees are calculated is a price change under Agreement Section 3.6.

5. Prepaid balance

(a) What makes up the balance. Customer's balance is made up of credits Customer has bought (by a top-up or a purchase in its account) and any promotional credits Meibel has granted under Section 6.

(b) What it pays for. The balance pays usage fees and LLM Vendor Fees. It does not pay platform fees, which are charged separately under the Agreement.

(c) Order of use. Promotional credits are used first, those expiring soonest first, then bought credits, oldest first.

(d) Timing. Usage is recorded as it happens but may be deducted from the balance after a short delay, so the balance can briefly go below zero. Any amount below zero is owed by Customer and is taken from the next top-up or charged to Customer's payment method.

(e) Bought credits. Bought credits expire 12 months after purchase. They are not refundable, except where Meibel charged in error, where Meibel ends the Platform for Customer other than for Customer's breach, or where the law requires a refund. Meibel will email Customer before bought credits expire.

6. Promotional credits

(a) Offers. Meibel may grant promotional credits, for example a credit at signup or a monthly credit while a payment method is on file. The amount, who is eligible, and when the credits expire are as stated in the offer when it is made: on Meibel's pricing page, in the Platform, or in an Order Form. A bonus given with a purchase of credits is a promotional credit that pays usage fees only, not LLM Vendor Fees.

(b) Expiry. If an offer does not say when its credits expire, they expire 30 days after they are granted. Credits granted for a particular month expire at the end of that month. Unused promotional credits do not carry over.

(c) No cash value. Promotional credits have no cash value. They cannot be sold, transferred, exchanged or refunded, and they are not returned when an account closes.

(d) Eligibility and misuse. Each organization may receive a signup offer once. An offer may require a business email address, a payment method on file, or both. Meibel may decline, reduce or withdraw promotional credits, and may suspend the accounts involved, where credits are sought through duplicate or related accounts, personal or disposable email addresses, or other misuse.

(e) Changes. Meibel may change or end any offer at any time. Credits already granted keep the terms they were granted under.

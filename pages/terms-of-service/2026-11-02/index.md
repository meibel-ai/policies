# Schedule 1: Usage and Metering

This Schedule explains how Meibel measures and bills usage of the Platform. What Meibel meters, the unit
for each item, how it is measured and its price are set out in the Price List (Agreement Section 3.1) or
in Customer's Order Form.

## 1. How usage is measured

(a) Usage is measured and recorded by the Meibel platform. Meibel's usage records are presumed accurate for
billing, unless they contain a manifest error or Customer shows otherwise in a dispute under Agreement
Section 3.7.

(b) Each metered item is billed in arrears, based on Customer's actual usage during the billing period (a
calendar month, unless the Order Form says otherwise). Metered items are billed independently of one
another unless the Price List or this Schedule says otherwise.

(c) Customer can see itemized usage by line item in the Platform.

(d) Meibel may bill usage on its best available data at the close of each billing period and correct any
variance on a later invoice, as a separate line, within 90 days after that period ends.

## 2. Metered items

(a) The Price List describes each metered item: what it covers, its unit and how it is measured. Meibel
may add, describe or retire metered items in the Price List under Agreement Section 3.6. Before retiring
an item priced in Customer's Order Form during its Subscription Term, Meibel will give at least 90 days'
notice and make available a replacement that does the same job at no higher price.

(b) If Meibel changes how an existing item is measured in a way that increases Customer's charges, Meibel
treats the change as a price increase under Agreement Section 3.6.

(c) For items priced in an Order Form, the Price List descriptions in effect on its Effective Date apply
for the Subscription Term, unless a later description lowers Customer's charges.

## 3. Included allowances and presentation

(a) A plan may include an allowance of a metered item each billing period. Usage within the allowance is not
charged. Unused allowance does not carry over unless the Order Form says so.

(b) Prices may be shown in a larger unit than the one an item is metered in, such as per million tokens or
per GB-month. Usage is always metered at the unit in the Price List.

## 4. Third-party model usage

(a) **Through Meibel.** When Customer uses a third-party model through Meibel's own provider accounts,
Customer pays for that usage as stated in the Price List or Order Form (**"LLM Vendor Fees"**). A price
stated as provider cost (or a percentage above it) uses the provider's published standard price for the
model at the time of use. LLM Vendor Fees are metered from the provider usage the Platform records for
Customer's requests and billed in arrears, separately from the platform fee.

(b) **Customer's own key.** When Customer connects a model provider with its own account or API key, the
provider charges Customer directly under Customer's agreement with it, and no LLM Vendor Fees apply to that
usage. Any Meibel charge for using a customer-supplied key is stated in the Price List.

(c) **Price changes.** Changes in a provider's own prices flow through to LLM Vendor Fees when they take
effect. A change Meibel makes to how LLM Vendor Fees are calculated is a price change under Agreement
Section 3.6.

## 5. Prepaid balance

(a) **What makes up the balance.** Customer's balance is made up of credits Customer has bought (by a
top-up or a purchase in its account) and any promotional credits Meibel has granted under Section 6.

(b) **What it pays for.** The balance pays usage fees and LLM Vendor Fees. It does not pay platform fees,
which are charged separately under the Agreement.

(c) **Order of use.** Promotional credits are used first, those expiring soonest first, then bought credits,
oldest first.

(d) **Timing.** Usage is recorded as it happens but may be deducted from the balance after a short delay, so
the balance can briefly go below zero. Any amount below zero is owed by Customer and is taken from the next
top-up or charged to Customer's payment method.

(e) **Bought credits.** Bought credits expire 12 months after purchase. They are not refundable, except
where Meibel charged in error, where Meibel ends the Platform for Customer other than for Customer's
breach, or where the law requires a refund. Meibel will email Customer before bought credits expire.

## 6. Promotional credits

(a) **Offers.** Meibel may grant promotional credits, for example a credit at signup or a monthly credit
while a payment method is on file. The amount, who is eligible, and when the credits expire are as stated
in the offer when it is made: on Meibel's pricing page, in the Platform, or in an Order Form. A bonus given
with a purchase of credits is a promotional credit that pays usage fees only, not LLM Vendor Fees.

(b) **Expiry.** If an offer does not say when its credits expire, they expire 30 days after they are
granted. Credits granted for a particular month expire at the end of that month. Unused promotional
credits do not carry over.

(c) **No cash value.** Promotional credits have no cash value. They cannot be sold, transferred, exchanged
or refunded, and they are not returned when an account closes.

(d) **Eligibility and misuse.** Each organization may receive a signup offer once. An offer may require a
business email address, a payment method on file, or both. Meibel may decline, reduce or withdraw
promotional credits, and may suspend the accounts involved, where credits are sought through duplicate or
related accounts, personal or disposable email addresses, or other misuse.

(e) **Changes.** Meibel may change or end any offer at any time. Credits already granted keep the terms
they were granted under.

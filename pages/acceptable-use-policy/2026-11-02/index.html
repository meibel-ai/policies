Acceptable Use Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

This policy applies to everyone who uses the Meibel platform under a customer's account. Breaching it can lead to suspension under Agreement Section 5.3.

Don't attack or overload the platform. Don't probe, scan or test its security without Meibel's written permission. Don't interfere with or disrupt its operation, or send traffic designed to degrade it for others.

Don't get around limits or metering. Don't evade usage limits, access controls or usage metering, for example by spreading one workload across accounts created to collect free credits or avoid limits.

Keep accounts to your organization. Don't share login credentials or API keys outside your organization, or let people outside it use your account except as your Users.

Don't use the platform to harm others. Don't upload or send malicious code. Don't use agents or workflows to attack other systems, including prompt-injection attacks aimed at other people's AI systems. Don't store, process or send material that is unlawful or infringes anyone's rights.

No unrelated computation. Don't use platform resources for work unrelated to the platform's purpose, such as cryptocurrency mining.

Follow model and data-source rules. Follow the policies of any third-party model or service you use through the platform, and the terms of the data sources you connect.

Follow the law. Don't use the platform in breach of applicable law, including export control and sanctions law.

Report suspected misuse to support@meibel.ai.
